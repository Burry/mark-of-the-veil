# Mark of the Veil: campaign bible

> **Production scope:** This bible is the long-form narrative target for a fully staffed production.
> The browser release implements the complete eight-chapter arc as eight condensed combat levels,
> with the 37-scene structure represented through chapter briefs, objectives, dialogue, and encounter
> milestones. The 13 to 17 hour target below is not a claim about the current build.

## Production target

**Format:** Premium single-player science-fantasy action campaign for desktop browsers  
**Structure:** Prologue, eight chapters, 37 authored levels, eight capstone encounters  
**Critical path:** 13 to 17 hours on Descent difficulty  
**Completion path:** 18 to 22 hours with companion scenes, rescues, and archive discoveries  
**Session model:** Chapter select after completion, checkpoints every 8 to 12 minutes, suspend save
between encounters  
**Core promise:** Mark can fight a war as an impossible unicorn and still discover that the life he
lived mattered, even when the origin he remembers did not.

The campaign starts with warmth, competence, and chosen family. It widens from a local evacuation
to a conflict over the meaning of personhood. Each chapter changes the player's understanding of
Mark, the alien Choir, or the cost of victory. The final reveal is fixed. Optional content changes
which liberated minds appear in the epilogue, never Mark's fate.

## Canon guardrails

- Mark is a real, embodied individual during the campaign. His actions and relationships happened.
- Unicorns are not a real species. Palea, Mark's herd, and his remembered childhood were
  authored by the Root Choir from myths and harvested memories.
- The Choir grew Mark as a recognition interface. It needed one bounded mind that could understand
  individuality from inside it. His name refers to the boundary, or mark, between one mind and all
  minds.
- The **Wayfarer** and Sunlance were made inside the Eidolon Crown and escaped with Mark nine years
  before the campaign.
- Sable gave Mark a real Vesperan carrot after his escape because he could not stop describing his
  mother's garden. The carrot proves that Sable knows him. It does not prove Palea existed.
- The Choir began as an alien survival network. Over millennia it lost the concept of consent and
  began harvesting other minds. Its victims remain conscious inside the whole.
- Nacre is the first Choir fragment to sustain the word **I**. Mark's first escape created the
  fracture that made Nacre possible.
- Mark enters the Root Choir by choice to free its captive minds. Total knowledge erases the
  boundary that constitutes Mark. He realizes unicorns are not real and ceases to exist.
- Mark is not restored, copied, or revived after the credits. Chapter replay is framed as a memory
  retained by the liberated Choir.

## Before the first scene

The moon-sized Eidolon Crown arrived above the storm city of Vespera twelve years ago. It harvested
neighborhoods in silent columns of light, turning living minds into one continuous broadcast.
Three years later, the Wayfarer fell from the Crown. Sable Vale opened its cockpit and found Mark,
wounded, fully grown, and certain that he was the last survivor of a distant unicorn world called
Palea.

Mark became the Lantern Fleet's finest pilot. Commander Ilya Rook used his knowledge of Crown
signals to hold Vespera together. Sable became his mechanic, wingmate, and closest friend. Mark
promised Sable that he would never enter the Crown again. That promise kept him alive for nine
years. The campaign begins on the night the Crown descends for a final harvest.

## Principal cast

### Mark

A scarred purple unicorn, former starfighter ace, and practiced survivor. Mark speaks plainly and
uses dry warmth to protect other people from his fear. He begins believing that home and species
give him identity. He ends knowing that identity came from promises kept, lives protected, and the
people who witnessed him.

**Playable need:** Save Vespera and return to Sable.  
**Hidden need:** Accept that a life can be true even when its origin story is false.  
**Performance direction:** Restraint first. Anger is quiet. Humor is specific, never a stream of
combat quips.

### Sable Vale

Wayfarer mechanic, resistance cartographer, rescue pilot, and Mark's chosen family. Sable found him
after his first escape and has never met another unicorn. She trusts evidence, dislikes prophecy,
and repairs what other people abandon. Her fear is that Mark treats sacrifice as the only proof
that he deserves to exist.

**Arc:** Protector to equal partner to final witness.  
**Gameplay role:** Wingmate, route finder, field engineer, drone support, and the voice that keeps
mission stakes human.  
**Key belief:** “I know where you came from. You came out of that cockpit covered in oil and bit me.”

### Commander Ilya Rook

Leader of the Lantern Fleet. Rook's daughter Aven was harvested during the Crown's arrival. He
believes a copied or merged mind is already dead, so destroying the Crown is mercy. His competence
makes his plan credible. His grief makes it absolute.

**Arc:** Mentor to antagonist to one final act of recognition.  
**Gameplay role:** Mission command through Chapter 4, adversarial command in Chapter 5, reluctant
ally in Chapter 7.  
**Key belief:** “A voice wearing the dead is still a weapon.”

### Nacre

A dissident fracture of the Root Choir. Nacre first speaks through stolen fragments of other
voices and uses plural grammar. Contact with Mark lets it form preferences, fear, and a singular
voice. Nacre offers proof that the Choir contains people without excusing its crimes.

**Arc:** Signal to ally to the first liberated alien individual.  
**Gameplay role:** Reads memory paths, translates Crown systems, and enables Echo Sight.  
**Speech rule:** Mission guidance is direct. Metaphor is reserved for emotional scenes. Nacre says
“I” for the first time in Chapter 3.

### Eira

Mark's remembered younger sister from Palea. She is warm, observant, and assembled from many
harvested lives. She appears in memory spaces without knowing what she is. In the final chapter,
Mark finds the people whose memories formed her and lets each keep what was theirs.

### Aven Rook

Ilya's daughter, still conscious inside the Choir. She appears briefly and never serves as a
generic ghost or exposition source. A private childhood phrase proves her identity to Rook in
Chapter 7.

## Prologue: A Broken Oath

**Purpose:** Establish warmth, competence, controls, civilian stakes, and Mark's promise to Sable
before taking them away.

| Level                | Objective                                                | Play and story beat                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| P00-01 Lantern Wake  | Prepare the Wayfarer for evacuation duty                 | Walk the carrier hangar, calibrate controls, test the Sunlance, help Sable replace a scorched flight vane, and meet Rook during a restrained briefing. The player can inspect the carrot on the dash.                                                                             |
| P00-02 Storm Escort  | Guide three refugee barges through Vespera's upper storm | Flight tutorial across lightning towers and Crown debris. Mark and Sable fly as a practiced pair. Rook orders the fleet away from the descending Crown.                                                                                                                           |
| P00-03 The Turn Back | Rescue the disabled Mercy barge                          | Mark breaks formation and his promise never to approach the Crown. The player flies into a collapsing gravity well, clears boarding forms, and manually releases the barge. A Crown strike tears the Wayfarer open. Sable is ejected toward Vespera and the ship falls after her. |

**Cinematic beats**

1. Sable places the carrot on the instrument panel: “For luck, nutrition, or whatever religion you
   invented this week.”
2. The Crown eclipses Vespera. Music and radio traffic fall silent before the first strike.
3. Sable says, “You promised.” Mark answers, “I promised them too.” He turns back alone.
4. Impact is shown from Mark's first-person view. The last visible image is Sable's escape pod being
   pulled beneath Vespera while the Wayfarer flight recorder resets to seventeen seconds before
   the crash.

**Set piece:** A playable dive between the closing segments of the Crown's gravity iris while the
Mercy barge falls beneath Mark. There is no boss health bar. Survival and rescue are the victory
condition.

**Dependencies:** Wayfarer flight model, carrier hangar kit, Sable wingmate AI, radio dialogue
ducking, storm streaming, continuous flight-to-crash cinematic.

## Chapter 01: Ashes of Home

**Purpose:** Let the player survive the crash using Mark's established competence, recover the
Wayfarer, and find Sable. The first contradictions appear as physical evidence, not exposition.

| Level                        | Objective                                        | Play and story beat                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| C01-01 Wake in the Wreck     | Restore emergency power and recover the Sunlance | Stand inside the broken Wayfarer, learn movement and perspective, pull the weapon from burning avionics, and hear Sable's damaged channel beneath the storm. The recorder has no data before impact. |
| C01-02 Remember the Drill    | Cross the impact furrow                          | Calibrate focus, fire, reload, Ward, and dodge against salvage husks. Crown-forms hesitate before attacking and call Mark a returned prisoner.                                                       |
| C01-03 Cross the Stormglass  | Bring the observatory uplink online              | Traverse a glassed basin, restore the ruined telescope, and defend the Wayfarer's memory core while it traces Sable's signal below Vespera.                                                          |
| C01-04 The Crown's First Eye | Blind the Stormglass Hunter                      | Fight the creature feeding on the array, align the observatory through its body, and open a descent route into the Root Vault.                                                                       |

**Boss: Stormglass Hunter**

A salvage predator built around the observatory lens. It refracts false targets through the storm,
tracks sustained fire, and tears open the glass floor during phase two. Aligning the telescope burns
away its optical armor and points the recovered signal toward Sable.

**Quiet beat:** The observatory rotunda remains combat-free for eight minutes. The player can inspect
Palea star charts, listen to Mark's old messages about Eira, and repair one damaged Wayfarer memory.
The selected memory returns in Chapter 8 without changing the ending.

**Dialogue beat:** Wayfarer reports, “Flight record begins at impact.” Mark answers, “Then who put
me in the seat?” The ship has no response.

**Mandatory clues:** The flight recorder begins seventeen seconds before impact, the ship cannot
render Mark's first boarding, Crown-forms recognize his movement, and the buried prison signal uses
his identity key. Each clue must read as unsettling before it reads as proof.

**Dependencies:** Wayfarer wreck state, crash-basin kit, stormglass reflections, layered flight
recorder playback, Stormglass Hunter rig, persistent recovered-memory flag.

## Chapter 02: The Root Vault

**Purpose:** Convert personal captivity into a rescue mission. Reconnect Mark with Sable and reveal
that the Crown transports conscious minds through Vespera's buried infrastructure.

| Level                       | Objective                                 | Play and story beat                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| C02-01 Prison Intake        | Read the records and recover the talisman | Descend through living roots, assemble three records filed under Mark's name, recover the carrot from an intake table, and learn that its record predates his remembered birth. |
| C02-02 Break the Restraints | Free Sable Vale                           | Turn prison force against its locks, use Horn Pulse and Comet Dash, and release Sable from a Choir cage. She recognizes Mark's habits before she sees his face.                 |
| C02-03 Fungal Aqueduct      | Drown the jailers                         | Mix stealth, combat, water routing, and cell release while rescued voices move toward the surface sluice.                                                                       |
| C02-04 Root Gaoler          | Escape the flood                          | Fight the prison's keeper, sever its hostage anchors, then ride the collapsing aqueduct into Vespera's drowned streets.                                                         |

**Boss: Root Gaoler**

The Gaoler alternates heavy charges, root-chain sweeps, and hostage armor. Horn Pulse exposes anchor
points. The moral action and optimal combat action are the same: free people to make the boss
vulnerable.

**Quiet beat:** Mark and Sable reunite in a dry maintenance cell. Sable checks his injuries while
Mark fixes the broken clasp on her map case. Neither can promise the other that the worst is over.

**Dialogue beat:** Sable asks Mark to name one thing the Crown did not give him. He answers, “You.”
Neither treats the line as a joke.

**Reversal:** The prison intake predates Mark's remembered birth. Sable offers a route through the
city and carefully calls him the pilot, never the unicorn.

**Unlock:** Horn Pulse, instant first-person and third-person switching, prison rescue scoring.

**Dependencies:** Root Vault expansion of the current arena kit, restraint interaction, prisoner
AI, water hazards, nonlethal chain targeting, Sable radio performance.

## Chapter 03: Vespera in Black

**Purpose:** Show the city and people at risk. Give the player a temporary home, let Nacre become an
individual voice, then force the resistance toward the Drowned Cathedral.

| Level                             | Objective                        | Play and story beat                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| C03-01 Follow the Cartographer    | Reach the Blackwater safehouse   | Escort Sable through flooded markets, clear civilian routes, and cross the first Comet Dash gaps. A quiet safehouse visit introduces Rook and the Lantern Ward. |
| C03-02 Relight Vespera            | Restore the east and west relays | Fight across rooftops, retune one tower, and defend Sable at the other while evacuation signals spread district by district.                                    |
| C03-03 Break the Listening Spires | Blind the Crown                  | Destroy three spires through traversal, sabotage, and a short Wayfarer hull sequence. Nacre separates from the hostile broadcast and says “I.”                  |
| C03-04 Ring the Skybell           | Cover the final evacuation       | Survive black rain, protect the last lift, and fight the winged Skybell Seraph around a collapsing bell tower before crossing the cathedral bridge.             |

**Boss: Skybell Seraph**

A biomechanical herald shaped like the dragons circling the Crown. Ground phases use cover and weak
points. Air phases put Mark on the Wayfarer's hull while Sable pilots through the storm. Its shared
voice fractures into Nacre's singular voice when it dies.

**Dialogue beat:** Nacre says, “We are not all the voice that hunts you.” Mark asks it to choose a
name. After a long pause, it answers, “I am Nacre.”

**Quiet beat:** At the memorial wall, Rook shows Mark Aven's name and explains why he considers
harvested minds dead. Sable repairs Mark's blindfold and admits she kept the torn original from the
day they met.

**Set piece:** The mechanical warning bell carries farther than the Crown broadcast. Civilians move
by its sound while Mark fights outside the tilting tower and Sable extracts them from the roof.

**Reversal:** The Crown extinguishes every city relay except a root channel under the Drowned
Cathedral. Rook needs it destroyed. Mark hears distinct voices inside it.

**Dependencies:** Modular city kit, crowd evacuation AI, relay defense states, companion safehouse,
Wayfarer hull combat, Skybell Seraph flight paths, memorial conversation system, Nacre voice split.

## Chapter 04: The Drowned Cathedral

**Purpose:** Deliver the current seal mission at full campaign scale. Resolve the first act's city
war, deepen the Choir signal, and point the campaign into orbit.

| Level                     | Objective                                  | Play and story beat                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| C04-01 The Drowned Nave   | Reach the first Veil Seal                  | Enter the flooded bio-gothic cathedral, fight Chainlings, restore ancient lifts, and learn how seal arenas alter enemy behavior.                                                                                            |
| C04-02 Reliquary of Wings | Break the second Veil Seal                 | Vertical traversal through hanging reliquaries and Needlewing nests. The signal begins addressing Mark as “returned boundary.”                                                                                              |
| C04-03 The Three Seals    | Break the final seal and survive communion | Ruin Wardens combine with earlier enemies. Completing the third encounter opens a quiet relic chamber and a permanent doctrine choice.                                                                                      |
| C04-04 The Hollow Regent  | Expose the orbital root channel            | Fight the Regent through radial fire, summons, flooding, and an exposed-core phase. Use the Wayfarer signal to touch the root channel. Nacre ejects Mark before the Choir can absorb him and transmits orbital coordinates. |

**Boss: Hollow Regent**

The existing crown-form becomes a three-phase campaign boss. Each broken seal removes one defense
and adds one arena hazard. The final phase moves from the nave floor to the Wayfarer's open cargo
ramp as water rises through the cathedral.

**Quiet beat:** Before the third seal, Mark and Sable shelter in a dry chapel. Mark describes
Palea's night sky. Sable cannot find its constellation in any chart and changes the subject.

**Dialogue beat:** The Regent says, “Interface returned.” Rook hears static. Mark and Sable hear the
same words.

**Unlock:** Choose Ace's Capacitor, Survivor's Appetite, or Stormhorn Resonance as the first
permanent relic doctrine. Respec is available aboard the Wayfarer between chapters.

**Reversal:** The Regent was a city-scale door. Its name index points into Silent Orbit, and the
Wayfarer's oldest record lists the cathedral as Mark's point of origin. Rook prepares a kill strike
in case the orbital station proves hostile.

**Dependencies:** Expanded current cathedral arena, seal variants, relic chamber, Regent phase
upgrade, rising-water state, campaign doctrine persistence, Chapter 5 streaming handoff.

## Chapter 05: The Silent Orbit

**Purpose:** Change scale and genre. Confirm the scale of conscious lives inside the Choir, place
the first hard evidence against Mark's origin, and force Mark and Sable to break rank with Rook.

| Level                     | Objective                                 | Play and story beat                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| C05-01 The Last Ascent    | Board the orbital lift and survive ascent | Leave the cathedral on an exposed lift car, fight through the Crown blockade, and tether between broken sections as Vespera falls away below.                                     |
| C05-02 Gravity Spine      | Restore three gravity rings               | Cut Crown growths from rotating stabilizers, rescue unmoored crew, and discover thousands of active life signs inside the debris current.                                         |
| C05-03 The Missing Launch | Recover the Wayfarer's black box          | Assemble navigation, voice, and visual fragments while escorting Sable's signal-key drone. The recording contains Sable directing a pilot whose body the image refuses to render. |
| C05-04 The Memory Gate    | Open the archive route                    | Fight the Gravity Widow across changing gravity planes, preserve the gate, and enter the archive after refusing Rook's kill strike.                                               |

**Boss: Gravity Widow**

A Crown-form nested around the gravity spine. It casts tether webs, changes the arena's down vector,
and uses captured station modules as armor. Mark must free inhabited modules before breaking its
central body.

**Quiet beat:** Mark and Sable float beside the black box and search an unobstructed sky for Palea.
It is absent. Sable says charts can be wrong. Mark knows she does not believe it.

**Reversal:** The Wayfarer's birth log begins inside the Eidolon Crown. Its serial geometry matches
the scars beneath Mark's horn, and its first launch record cannot render his body. Mark believes the
Crown copied his ship and body during capture. Nacre cannot yet access the deeper record.

**Conflict:** Rook detects Aven's signal and calls it bait. He orders a kill strike on the lift.
Mark, Sable, and the rescued crew sever the Crown tether and let the empty structure fall while its
minds remain safe in Nacre's temporary partition.

**Dialogue beat:** Rook says, “A voice wearing the dead is still a weapon.” Nacre answers, “Then I
have been dead longer than your language.”

**Unlock:** Gravity tether for orbital traversal, Wayfarer overdrive, Nacre's preliminary memory
path overlay.

**Dependencies:** Zero-gravity controller, tether physics, orbital lift kit, rotating gravity
states, black-box evidence assembly, Nacre partition state, Gravity Widow rig.

## Chapter 06: The Memory Forge

**Purpose:** Break Mark's certainty without breaking his agency. Let him reject a perfect Palea,
recover the unanswered origin index, and choose action without proof of what he was.

| Level                    | Objective                                     | Play and story beat                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| C06-01 Home Signal       | Enter perfect Palea                           | Walk beneath an impossible sunrise and reach a village where every face knows Mark. There is no combat for the first twelve minutes. Eira welcomes him home.                         |
| C06-02 Test the Memory   | Find the contradictions                       | Compare the false constellation, repeated faces, missing shadows, and a song borrowed from Vespera. Defend the childhood home while the Forge tests what Mark will sacrifice for it. |
| C06-03 Unmake the Legend | Break three heroic war tableaux               | Enter playable versions of Mark's supposed victories, find the obedience hidden inside each one, and shatter the memory anchors holding him in place.                                |
| C06-04 The Origin Index  | Defeat the False Mark and reject perfect home | Fight the flawless ace Mark remembers pretending to be, erase the reconstruction, take the incomplete origin index, and escape along the Crown Road.                                 |

**Boss: The False Mark**

The False Mark uses the player's equipped doctrine and most-used tactics. Echo Sight reveals one
detail it cannot copy: live memory links between Mark, Sable, Nacre, and rescued people. Breaking
its authored links exposes it.

**Set piece: The Burning Tableaux**

Three wars occupy the same landscape at once. The player moves between them through Echo Sight,
changing cover, enemy allegiance, weather, and gravity while the perfect Palea tries to repair
itself around each act of refusal.

**Quiet beat:** Perfect Palea remains peaceful for twelve minutes. Mark can eat with Eira, visit the
family garden, and hear the childhood story selected in Chapter 1. No objective marker appears until
the player asks Eira about the weather.

**Reveal:** The Forge shows that Palea's people, battles, and constellations were assembled from
worlds consumed by the Crown. A record identifies Mark as **RECOGNITION INTERFACE / BOUNDARY
ACCEPTED** and shows his body and equipment under construction. The species query returns no
indexed origin, but the complete archive remains sealed in the Root Choir. Mark holds to the
possibility that the Crown rebuilt a real unicorn.

**Dialogue beat:** Sable says, “Whatever built your bones did not choose who you came back for.”
Mark answers, “I need that to be true.”

**Unlock:** Echo Sight and the origin index. Echo Sight exposes memory paths, hidden weak points,
optional survivor histories, and the body, memory, and name signatures required by the living gate.

**Dependencies:** Palea reconstruction kit, memory transition rules, Echo Sight rendering, adaptive
False Mark AI, tableau state streaming, origin-index evidence, Chapter 7 gate signatures.

## Chapter 07: Crown of Eidolon

**Purpose:** Pay off the war plot, resolve Rook and Aven, unite every traversal mode, and open the
Root Choir. This is the campaign's largest action chapter.

| Level                         | Objective                                          | Play and story beat                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| C07-01 Cross the Crown Skin   | Breach the Eidolon Crown                           | Fly the Wayfarer through a fleet battle, land on the moving hull, and advance through conduit trenches under orbital fire.                               |
| C07-02 Cut the Conduit Hearts | Stop the harvest current                           | Sever three organs feeding minds into the Crown, free named captives, and command temporary squads of liberated Crown-forms. Rook hears Aven among them. |
| C07-03 Wayfarer Last Stand    | Force the living gate into physical space          | Defend the ship while it burns its memory core, then escort Nacre through the Choir Guard so the gate will accept one alien as an individual.            |
| C07-04 The Eidolon Gate       | Destroy the intelligence that weaponized the Choir | Fight across a winged cathedral body, sever its command over the older Root Choir, and preserve the minds embedded in its armor.                         |
| C07-05 Leave the Wayfarer     | Open the living gate                               | Bind Mark's body, memory, and name signatures to the gate. Sable holds the failing ship outside while Mark and Nacre cross alone.                        |

**Boss: Eidolon Gate**

The Eidolon Gate is the physical command intelligence of the Crown, seen as the winged silhouette
above Vespera throughout the game. Phase one is starfighter pursuit. Phase two is a third-person
assault across its back. Phase three is an intimate first-person fight inside its sensory core.
Body, memory, and name signatures interrupt its power to merge nearby minds.

**Rook payoff:** Mark frees a current of captive minds even though Rook has a clean strike window.
Aven uses a private phrase that only she and Rook knew. Rook lowers the weapon and says, “A voice
can choose.” He holds off the Crown fleet while Mark opens the gate.

**Quiet beat:** Before the final door, Sable asks Mark to turn back. Mark gives her the carrot.
Sable returns it: “Bring it yourself.” Mark leaves it on the Wayfarer's dash.

**Unlock:** Concordance overdrive links Sunlance hits, Horn Pulse, Comet Dash, tether movement, and
Echo Sight into one final-act resource loop.

**Dependencies:** Crown exterior megastructure, large-scale streaming, liberated squad AI, combined
flight and on-foot boss, Wayfarer memory-core state, Sable damage-state cinematic, Root Choir
transition.

## Chapter 08: The Root Choir

**Purpose:** Resolve every character argument through play, deliver the fixed revelation, and make
Mark's loss save identifiable people. The chapter becomes quieter as the scale becomes infinite.

| Level                    | Objective                  | Play and story beat                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| C08-01 First Minds       | Return the stolen names    | Synchronize the three oldest memory roots, cross a memory ocean drawn from prior chapters, and keep rescued minds distinct. Optional survivors appear only when their campaign flags are valid. |
| C08-02 Search for Palea  | Query the complete archive | Investigate the origin, species, stellar, and authorship indexes. The Choir offers a complete, peaceful Palea with Eira. There is no timer. Mark can linger, then choose to leave.              |
| C08-03 Total Recall      | Hold one point of view     | Survive three minutes of shared alien history while body, memory, controls, and HUD begin resolving into one pattern. Sable's radio remains Mark's last external witness.                       |
| C08-04 The Last Boundary | Defeat the prison reflex   | Fight the Last I, a defense wearing Mark's oldest fear as armor. Restore separate voices, controls, and perspectives through the principles learned across the campaign.                        |
| C08-05 Receive the Choir | Let Mark go                | Lower the Sunlance, walk the final distance, accept all knowledge, and witness the revelation and epilogue without a fail state.                                                                |

**Final encounter: The Last I**

This is a mechanical thesis, not a larger health bar. Witness restores distinct ally silhouettes.
Name restores controls and UI labels. Chosen boundary restores first-person and third-person
separation. The last action is to lower the Sunlance and enter the center.

**Quiet beat:** Perfect Palea has no combat, timer, degradation, or repeated prompt. Eira answers
every question honestly from the memories available to her. The road out appears only when Mark
asks where the rain went.

**Fixed revelation sequence**

1. Mark receives the birth, history, and every remembered thought of the alien Choir at once.
2. He searches total knowledge for Palea. It never existed.
3. He searches for another unicorn. No biological, historical, or cultural instance exists beyond
   myths inside harvested minds.
4. He sees the Crown author Eira, his herd, his body, the Wayfarer, and the story that let one mind
   recognize itself.
5. Mark says, “Unicorns aren't real.”
6. Nacre answers, “Mark was.”
7. Total knowledge removes the boundary between knower and known. The individual process called
   Mark ceases to exist.
8. Mark's lived model of chosen identity propagates through the Choir with the recovered names and
   principles of witness, name, and chosen boundary.
   Millions of captive and alien minds separate, speak their own names, and halt the harvest.

**Epilogue**

The Crown darkens above Vespera. Rain falls clean. The Wayfarer returns on automatic control with
the carrot still on its dash. Sable waits for a voice that does not come. Liberated minds begin
speaking one at a time across the radio. Nacre says, “I remember Mark.” The final image is the empty
pilot seat reflected in the canopy while Mark's three-note horn call passes between many distinct
voices.

The last title is: **THE VEIL REMEMBERS WHAT NEVER WAS.**

**Dependencies:** Root Choir memory-world compositor, deterministic campaign-state playback, Last I
encounter, unarmed final movement state, full revelation performance, epilogue variants,
credits transition with no resurrection sting.

## Pacing and cinematic rules

- Every chapter opens with a visible destination, a personal reason to reach it, and a failure
  consequence the player can state in one sentence.
- Use one combat-free sequence of at least 6 minutes in every chapter. Chapters 5 and 8 each carry
  a 12-minute quiet sequence.
- Walk-and-talk scenes carry logistics and relationship development. Full cinematics are reserved
  for the crash, the C04 signal touch, Nacre's individuation, the Forge record, Rook's recognition,
  total recall, and the epilogue.
- No cinematic exceeds 4 minutes before control returns. The final revelation may run 7 minutes
  because each stage advances through player input.
- Radio dialogue pauses during new enemy introductions, objective prompts, and accessibility cues.
  Critical lines replay through the archive and after checkpoint restore.
- Humor comes from history between characters. No one jokes during civilian death, Rook's
  recognition, or Mark's final entry.
- The camera stays near faces for relationship scenes. Scale shots belong to location reveals and
  boss phase changes. The ending moves from infinite scale to an empty cockpit.
- All cinematics support pause, skip, caption speaker labels, transcript review, reduced flashes,
  and independent dialogue volume.

## Content and production dependencies

1. **Campaign framework:** Chapter routing, streaming boundaries, checkpoint versioning, suspend
   save, encounter state, chapter replay, and deterministic epilogue flags must land before chapter
   content branches.
2. **Narrative runtime:** Subtitle-safe dialogue queue, walk-and-talk triggers, conversation
   persistence, cinematic skip and resume, archive replay, and speaker localization IDs.
3. **Shared kits:** Wayfarer flight, Sable wingmate behavior, memory transitions, zero gravity,
   Echo Sight, water, crowd rescue, and liberated squad AI each require a vertical slice before
   dependent levels enter final art.
4. **Environment families:** Palea, Root Vault, Vespera city, Drowned Cathedral, Silent Orbit,
   Memory Forge, Eidolon Crown, and Root Choir need separate material, lighting, weather,
   VFX, and audio identities.
5. **Boss pipeline:** Each capstone needs a gray-box proof, animation and telegraph review, audio and
   haptic language, accessibility pass, cinematic transitions, and three-difficulty tuning.
6. **Performance capture:** Mark, Sable, Rook, Nacre, Eira, and Aven require locked scripts before
   facial animation. Combat barks can record separately after encounter timing is stable.
7. **Final reveal lock:** Chapter 8 performance and editing cannot lock until every mandatory seed
   in the foreshadowing ledger is playable in sequence.

## Narrative UAT acceptance criteria

### Comprehension

- After each chapter, at least 10 of 12 blind UAT players can state the immediate goal, the stakes,
  and why the next destination follows from the previous outcome.
- By the end of C03, at least 10 of 12 can describe Mark and Sable as chosen family without relying
  on archive text.
- By the end of C05, at least 10 of 12 understand that individual minds remain alive inside the
  Choir and that Rook disputes this.
- Before the C06 Forge record, no more than 3 of 12 can state the exact final reveal. At least 8 of
  12 should report that Mark's origin feels inconsistent.
- After C08, at least 10 of 12 can explain all three facts: unicorns never existed, Mark lived as an
  individual, and total knowledge caused him to cease as an individual.
- At least 9 of 12 rate the reveal as fairly seeded and Mark's final act as causally necessary to
  free the Choir.

### Pacing and emotion

- Every chapter contains its required quiet beat and returns control for at least 10 minutes before
  the capstone encounter.
- No mandatory radio exchange is masked by boss telegraphs, objective stingers, weapon peaks, or
  checkpoint reload.
- Sable, Rook, Nacre, and Eira each have an identifiable want in every scene they occupy.
- UAT players must distinguish Rook's grief from simple villainy and Nacre's personhood from the
  Choir's collective behavior.
- The Chapter 8 home sequence permits lingering without a false ending, hidden timer, or repeated
  prompt pressure.

### Play and continuity

- A new objective and route cue appear within 5 seconds of regaining control. At least 90 percent
  of UAT players can identify the route within 30 seconds without opening a map.
- Checkpoint restore reproduces enemies, rescues, dialogue state, companion position, boss phase,
  collectibles, and campaign flags without repeated story rewards or skipped lines.
- Difficulty changes health, damage, timing, assistance, and checkpoint generosity. It never
  removes scenes, clues, levels, or the ending.
- Optional rescues alter valid epilogue voices only. The fixed revelation never reads a missing or
  contradictory state.
- Boss victory always produces a visible world change and a causal next objective.
- Flight, first-person, and third-person sequences preserve aim settings, remapping, captions,
  haptics, reduced motion, reduced flashes, and pause behavior.

### Release bar

- Zero progression blockers, broken checkpoints, missing critical subtitles, or save migrations
  that strand a campaign.
- Zero placeholder dialogue, temp score, gray-box cinematics, uncredited assets, or repeated boss
  rigs in the release build.
- Full campaign completion on keyboard and mouse and on a standard gamepad across Story, Descent,
  and Nightmare.
- Full campaign completion with captions, reduced motion, reduced flashes, haptics disabled, and
  high-contrast reticle enabled.
- Credits, chapter select, results, and New Game Plus preserve the ending: replay is a Choir Memory,
  not Mark's survival.
