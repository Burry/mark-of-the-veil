# Game design: Mark of the Veil

## Premise

Mark is a scarred anthropomorphic purple unicorn, former starfighter ace, and the last pilot able
to breach the Eidolon Crown, a moon-sized machine turning Vespera's living minds into a broadcast.
His fighter, the **Wayfarer**, is shot down over a storm-wrapped city. Mark is captured beneath the
ruins, escapes with his battered Sunlance and a carrot kept as a reminder of home, then lights the
three seals that can guide the Wayfarer back to him.

The release is one complete replayable mission. Its tone is earnest cosmic fantasy with dry warmth.
The carrot works as both a recovery relic and a character beat.

## Run structure

1. **The Root Vault:** break the last restraint, reclaim the carrot, learn move/look/fire/dash,
   and step into the drowned cathedral-city.
2. **The Three Seals:** clear three escalating encounters and activate each signal seal.
3. **Relic Communion:** select one run upgrade.
4. **The Hollow Regent:** survive projectile patterns and adds, then break the exposed core.
5. **The Root Choir:** use the Wayfarer signal to infiltrate the exposed alien hive-mind.
6. **The Revelation:** Mark receives the Choir's total knowledge and discovers that unicorns
   never existed: his body, species, and remembered home were an interface authored by the prison.
   With no boundary left between knower and knowledge, Mark ceases to exist. The Choir retains his
   memory; the player receives time, accuracy, kills, score, damage-taken, and C–S rank.

Normal difficulty targets 10–15 minutes for a first successful run. Story and Nightmare scale
incoming damage, enemy speed, and pressure without changing the authored mission.

## Combat kit

- **Sunlance carbine:** accurate automatic relic weapon, 36-round magazine, reload, hitscan impact,
  recoil, muzzle light, and a distinct mechanical/crystalline audio signature.
- **Comet Dash:** omnidirectional burst with a short invulnerability window and a 3-second cooldown.
- **Horn Pulse:** expanding close-range shockwave that damages and knocks back enemies; 12-second
  cooldown.
- **Ward and Resolve:** a 50-point regenerating shield over 100 health. Shield begins recovering
  after Mark avoids damage for four seconds.
- **Perspective:** third person emphasizes Mark's silhouette and spatial awareness; first person
  tightens the encounter around purple-furred forearms, weapon, and horn-energy effects.

## Enemies

- **Chainling:** fast quadrupedal hunter; crouch and chain-rattle telegraph its lunge.
- **Needlewing:** airborne standoff unit; a coral targeting wedge precedes its plasma spine.
- **Ruin Warden:** armored heavy; dodge the charge and attack the bright rear core.
- **Hollow Regent:** levitating broken-crown boss with radial shots, shockwaves, summon windows, and
  a vulnerable central pearl.

Every dangerous action communicates through shape, motion, sound, color, optional caption, and
optional haptic cue. No threat relies on color alone.

## Relic upgrades

- **Ace's Capacitor:** 48-round belt, harder-hitting shots, faster carbine fire, and quicker reloads.
- **Survivor's Appetite:** higher maximum Resolve and Ward, plus gradual Resolve recovery once
  incoming pressure has relented long enough for Ward regeneration.
- **Stormhorn Resonance:** wider, harder-hitting Horn Pulse with a substantially shorter cooldown.

## Scoring and persistence

Fast kills build a multiplier up to 4×. Health damage breaks the chain. Rank combines completion,
score, accuracy, and damage taken; elapsed time is recorded and breaks ties between otherwise equal
best runs. Settings and the best run are stored locally; no account or backend is required.

## Deliberate release boundaries

No multiplayer, dialogue trees, inventory grid, character creator, touch-first controls, flight
simulation, or multi-level open world. The opening crash and final neural integration are cinematic
beats using the same authored assets. This concentrates production on combat clarity, camera feel,
atmosphere, accessibility, and replay polish.
